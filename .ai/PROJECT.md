## Purpose
VidyaDesk is the unified school project hub. It connects existing school-owned applications and services through a consistent workspace without duplicating their data ownership or presenting unverified integrations as operational.

## Scope
- Project overview and launch points for owner repositories.
- Read-only integration catalogue and aggregate-only Report Central surface.
- Server-side integration endpoints that remain disabled until their source, school scope, authentication and security gates are verified.
- Gradual integration of existing owner projects through documented contracts.

## Explicit exclusions
VidyaDesk is not currently the system of record for students, staff, attendance, fees, timetables or exams. Do not add generic CRUD modules without an approved owner project and verified data contract.
